# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Core Foundation (MVP)
- Inventory ledger, item master, multi-location stock
- Purchase and sales workflows
- Basic reporting and role-based access

## Phase 2 — Industry Config + Admin
- Industry templates and configuration studio
- Tenant provisioning and white-label branding
- Workflow engine and dynamic forms

## Phase 3 — Finance + HR
- Financial analytics and P&L
- HR module and workforce management
- Advanced approvals and audit trails

## Phase 4 — AI/BI Expansion
- Forecasting, smart replenishment, anomaly detection
- Self-serve BI and narrative insights
- Model governance and monitoring

## Phase 5 — Ecosystem & Marketplace
- Integrations marketplace and partner APIs
- Reseller management and revenue share
- Vertical solution packs

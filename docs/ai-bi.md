# BI & AI Capabilities

## Business Intelligence
- **Executive Dashboard**: revenue, margin, cash flow, and inventory turns.
- **Operational KPIs**: fill rate, stockouts, aging inventory, supplier performance.
- **Financial Analytics**: P&L, variance analysis, cost center reporting.
- **Self-Serve BI**: ad hoc queries, custom dashboards, export pipelines.

## AI-Driven Features
- **Demand Forecasting**: time-series models for SKU/location forecasts.
- **Smart Replenishment**: safety stock recommendations and reorder points.
- **Anomaly Detection**: detect shrinkage, abnormal sales, or inventory drift.
- **Predictive Maintenance**: asset utilization and failure risk (where applicable).
- **Automated Narratives**: explain KPI changes with natural language summaries.

## Data Pipeline
- **Feature Store**: shared features for forecasting and classification.
- **Model Registry**: versioned models with approval workflows.
- **Monitoring**: drift detection, data quality checks, and alerting.

## Governance
- **Explainability**: model insights, feature importance, confidence scores.
- **Human-in-the-Loop**: approval workflows for auto-generated recommendations.
